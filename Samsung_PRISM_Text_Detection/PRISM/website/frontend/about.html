How OmniDetect Works

The Challenge

Early AI detectors relied on simple metrics like TF-IDF or basic burstiness. Modern LLMs (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini) have learned to mimic human writing patterns, rendering old detectors obsolete. They produce varied sentence lengths, use diverse vocabulary, and can adopt informal tones.

Our Solution: Multi-Signal Ensemble

OmniDetect combines three independent analytical engines to form a single, robust ensemble prediction, making it much harder for AI to evade detection without failing on at least one signal.

1. Enhanced Stylometry

Extracts 30+ statistical features from the text (Burstiness coefficient, MATTR, Hapax legomena ratio, etc.) and uses a Gradient Boosting Classifier to find complex non-linear patterns.

2. Perplexity & Surprisal Diversity

Feeds the text through a local language model (DistilGPT-2). AI text is generally more predictable to other AI models, resulting in lower perplexity and distinct surprisal distributions (variance, skewness, kurtosis).

3. Linguistic Patterns

Rule-based detection of AI-typical habits: formulaic transitions ("In conclusion", "Moreover"), overconfident phrasing, hedging, and highly structured formatting.

Samsung PRISM Project

This platform was developed as part of a Samsung PRISM project focused on synthetic data detection. The goal is to provide a robust, explainable tool for distinguishing human creativity from algorithmic generation.
